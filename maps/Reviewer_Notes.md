# Atlas reviewer notes

The cards use the enhanced lore review and retain its era-specific framing. The attached research lists sources by location. Bethesda and ZeniMax publications support the regional settings and game premises. Prima’s published quest guides now support the Morthal burned-house investigation, Dawnstar’s Nightcaller Temple, Riften’s Ratway and Ragged Flagon, and Windhelm’s landmarks. In-game text and official patch notes anchor the Sea of Pearls entry. Community-hosted transcriptions are identified by host, and some finer lore details still rely on community references.
