The north star

A guest house with a story

Not a themed room assembled from props—a full-scale, hand-built retreat with the warmth, weight, and character of an old Nordic inn.

Rough timber. Firelight. Carved knotwork. A place that feels discovered rather than decorated.

The friendly innkeeper

The Mogollon Monster is the hearth's smiling mascot. A monster doesn't have to be mean—this one raises a mug in welcome.
